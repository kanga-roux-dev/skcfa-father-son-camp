How Points Are Awarded

Giant Swing YMCA Led

  • 20 ptsFor anyone who swings with no hands on the strop

Checkpoint 4 — Radio Rogaine YMCA Led · Max 1.5 Hours

Teams will be provided with a map, radio, phonetic alphabet and question sheet. Combine the scores for each Rogaine team to put on the group's scorecard.

  • 10 ptsFor every marker found and radioed in correctly using the phonetic alphabet
  • 10 ptsFor every question answered correctly
  • 5 ptsFor radioing in a marker without the correct radio procedure or phonetic alphabet

Checkpoint 5 — Hut Building Group Led · 1 Hour

In nature, the group works as a team to build a hut. Huts need to be built on the trees on the high side of the area — not near the mountain bike trail at the bottom of camp.

  • 10 ptsIf you can create a hut
  • 20 ptsIf the whole group can all sit in the hut
  • 50 ptsIf your hut is waterproof and the group doesn't get wet

St Kevin's College Fathers Association — Camp Packing Guide

Year 7–8 Father / Male Mentor & Son Camp  |  24th/25th October 2026  |  Anglesea Discovery Camp

Campers are responsible for their belongings while at camp. All items must be clearly named. See you soon — the YMCA Anglesea Discovery Camp team!

Mandatory Items
  • Backpack
  • Hat
  • Drink Bottle
Bedding
  • Sleeping Bag
  • Sheets / Pillow Slips
  • Bath Towel
Other
  • Camera
  • Torch
  • Bag for laundry
Clothing
  • Socks, Underwear
  • Pyjamas
  • Shirts (including some long sleeves)
  • Shorts (weather dependent)
  • Tracksuit Pants
  • Jumper / Jacket
  • Raincoat (puffer jackets are not waterproof)
  • Swimwear — for water activities
Footwear
  • Closed toe shoes / runners for activities and around camp
  • Spare old shoes (not crocs) for water activities
  • Thongs (for showering only, not to be worn around camp)
Personal Items
  • Towel
  • Toiletries — hair brush, hair ties, shampoo, soap, toothbrush & toothpaste
  • Deodorant (roll-on)
  • Insect repellent (roll-on)
  • Sunscreen (roll-on)
  • Sunglasses
What NOT to Pack
  • Aerosols — deodorant, insect repellent & sunscreen must be roll-on
  • Crocs, sandals etc. — not permitted around camp or for activities

St Kevin's College Fathers Association — Thank You

Thank You
For Attending
Anglesea Discovery Camp

The SKCFA is a vibrant and energetic group of fathers/guardians at St Kevin's, aiming to:

  • Foster a sense of community amongst the parents of St Kevin's College; and
  • Contribute to the development of the boys' relationship with their fathers/guardians through organised activities all year round.

The SKCFA contributes to every year level via events held for students and for every father/male mentor via e.g. dinners. We also help at school functions, again, being an active part of the college community.

If you too want to take part in your son's year level events, or other college functions, please reach out to us:
fathersassociation@stkevins.vic.edu.au

Keep up to date with all our activities via the college portal and our Facebook page:
https://www.facebook.com/groups/SKCFathersAssociation/
